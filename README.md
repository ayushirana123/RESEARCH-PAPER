AI-Based Flash Flood Prediction Using Deep Learning — A Bibliometric Study (2019–2025)

This repository contains my research paper, Turnitin Similarity Report (1%), and AI Detection Report (0%).
The study presents a detailed bibliometric analysis of global research on AI and Deep Learning-based flash flood prediction, with a special focus on mountainous regions like Uttarakhand, India.

About the Research

Flash floods are a major threat in hilly regions due to extreme rainfall, glacier bursts, and steep terrain.
This research explores how AI, Deep Learning models (LSTM, Neural Networks), and remote sensing technologies are helping improve early flood prediction.

The study uses VOSviewer to analyze:

* Research publications (2019–2025)
* Author networks
* Keyword clusters
* Co-citation relationships
* Trending research themes
* Research gaps in AI-based forecasting for mountainous regions

This work is one of the first bibliometric studies focused specifically on AI-based flash flood prediction in hilly areas.


Key Insights

* Journals such as Water and Journal of Hydrology are the most influential in this field.
* Most frequent keywords: flash flood, deep learning, LSTM, hydrology.
* Strong global collaboration networks, especially between Asian and European researchers.
* Main research gap: very limited studies combine AI with rainfall and river level data for mountainous terrain.
* Future scope includes IoT sensor networks, satellite imaging, and larger datasets.


Tools and Methods

* VOSviewer (Version 1.6.20) for bibliometric mapping
* Google Scholar and journal databases (2019–2025)
* CSV metadata extraction
* Analysis of ten major peer-reviewed research articles
